

# Privacy Policy for Books at Home

**Last updated: January 2025**

## Overview

Books at Home ("the App") is developed by Rahul Kadyan. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

## Data Collection

Books at Home does **not** collect, transmit, or share your personal data with the developer or any third parties. All your data stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud account.

### Data You Provide

* Book information (titles, authors, ISBNs, covers, etc.)
* Reading progress and status
* Personal notes and ratings
* Lending records (borrower names, due dates)
* Wishlist items

### Data Storage

* **Local Storage**: Your library data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData.
* **iCloud Sync**: If enabled, data syncs across your devices via your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data is protected by your Apple ID and iCloud security.

## Library Sharing

When you share your library with others via iCloud:

* Only book metadata is shared (titles, authors, covers, ratings, reading status)
* **Private data is excluded**: personal notes, lending information, purchase prices, and storage locations are never shared

## Third-Party Services

* **Apple iCloud/CloudKit**: Used for sync and sharing (governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/))
* **Book Data APIs**: When searching for books, queries are sent to book lookup services to retrieve metadata

## Analytics & Tracking

Books at Home does **not** include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Your Rights

You can:

* Export all your data via CSV at any time
* Delete all your data from within the App
* Disable iCloud sync in device settings

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please use the support page or contact via GitHub.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date.
